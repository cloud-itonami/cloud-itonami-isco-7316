# Business Model: Sign & Decorative Craft Workshop Coordination Practice

## Classification

- Repository: `cloud-itonami-isco-7316`
- ISCO-08: `7316`
- Occupation: Signwriters, Decorative Painters, Engravers and Etchers
- Social impact: worker-safety, craft-quality, production-throughput

## Customer

- sign shops, decorative-painting studios and engraving/etching workshops
- independent signwriting/decorative-craft crews / crew cooperatives

## Offer

- crew shift/task scheduling coordination
- job/commission/progress-record logging
- paint/solvent/etching-materials supply-order coordination
- safety-concern surfacing to shop safety officers

## Revenue

- monthly retainer
- per-crew coordination fee

## Trust Controls

- no direct finalization of a painting/engraving-execution decision
  (e.g. a specific signwriting, decorative-painting, engraving or
  etching procedure), ever
- no direct finalization of a workshop-chemical-safety-clearance
  decision, ever
- no override of a shop safety officer's judgment, ever
- flagged safety concerns (paint/solvent VOC exposure, etching-
  chemical handling, precision hand-tool/equipment condition) always
  route to human sign-off, regardless of confidence
- no supply order above the registered cost threshold without
  governor-gated human sign-off
- operating and coordination records are auditable, not editable
